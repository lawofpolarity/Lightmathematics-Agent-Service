## Consequence

## Remaining eligible frontier

The next benchmark must no longer manufacture a difference by choosing a weak
comparator. It must test cases where the challenge is *semantic dependency
formation or obligation preservation under transformation*, not ordinary graph
reachability.

Candidate Stage 4D classes:

1. **negative/frontier dependency** — a decision depends on the absence of a
   qualifying fact within a declared bounded universe;
2. **authority-evidence interaction** — evidence is valid generally but invalid
   for a particular operation/scope after a policy transition;
3. **unresolved-obligation transfer** — transformation produces a fluent output
   while an upstream unresolved condition must remain attached;
4. **selective retraction/re-derivation** — a changed premise invalidates only the
   affected reliance closure;
5. **projection after composition** — individually valid fragments compose into
   a representation that loses a decision-relevant distinction.

The strongest conventional comparator must receive the same source corpus,
bounded universe, rules/policies, update stream and ordinary inference
capabilities.

If it reproduces LM, the result remains NULL and the differentiation claim
contracts again.
