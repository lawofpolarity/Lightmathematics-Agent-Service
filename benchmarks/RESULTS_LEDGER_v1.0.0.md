## Combined interpretation

The elementary five-state decision mapping is reproducible by ordinary
conventional policy logic when supplied with the same state. Therefore the
decision labels and precedence rules are not evidence of an LM-specific
computational advantage.

Projection tests establish a different point: if a representation erases a
distinction that changes the correct reliance decision, the missing distinction
cannot be uniquely reconstructed from the collapsed representation alone.
Irrelevant changes do not justify refusal.

The surviving experimental frontier is therefore upstream of the final decision
label: preservation, discovery, validation and transfer of decision-relevant
state across mutation, projection, handoff and incomplete knowledge.
